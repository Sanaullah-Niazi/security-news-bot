### Critical Brotli Flaws Trigger Emergency Patches Across PHP, Node.js, and .NET

A severe memory corruption issue in the Brotli compression library—dubbed HTTP2Shell or Broccoli2Shell—has prompted urgent security updates across platforms including PHP, Microsoft .NET, and Node.js. Attackers have reportedly begun moving from initial scans to active exploitation in the wild, launching functional command shells. System administrators and developers should update affected runtime packages immediately to block remote attacks.

### Stealthy JavaScript Attack Compromises Nearly 1,000 E-Commerce Stores

Threat actors have injected evasive malicious code into 953 online storefronts, primarily those powered by Magento, in an attack campaign active since mid-2025. The hidden loader serves scripts to shoppers while deliberately avoiding automated scanners to evade detection. Merchants running Magento should inspect their site scripts, while consumers should closely monitor credit card statements for fraudulent charges.

### Cyber Incident Forces New Mexico Hospital to Divert Patients and Close Clinics

The University of New Mexico Health System instructed patients to avoid its primary Albuquerque facility and closed more than 30 clinics following a major cybersecurity incident. The disruption forced the hospital system to divert patients requiring care and cancel numerous scheduled appointments. Patients needing non-emergency assistance are advised to check provider announcements before traveling to clinics.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/cybersecurity-issue-forces-a-major-new-mexico-hospital-to-divert-patients-and-close-clinics/)</span>

### Scammers Impersonate IT Support in Microsoft Teams to Deliver Ransomware

Attackers are taking advantage of external chat features in Microsoft Teams to impersonate help desk staff and trick employees into downloading malicious software. Researchers warn that this social engineering technique is recurring across hundreds of incidents to deploy backdoors and ransomware. Workplaces should restrict unsolicited external messaging and verify all technical support requests through trusted internal channels.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-use-microsoft-teams-it.html)</span>

### AI Pentesting Framework Abused to Steal Over 8 Million Records

Police in South Korea identified seven suspects linked to an operation that breached over 90 organizations using the ARTEX AI penetration testing tool. The ring, reportedly led by a former security pentester, stole approximately 8.2 million records across affected databases. Organizations using automated security tools should enforce strict credential boundaries and audit the permissions granted to AI agents.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/former-pentester-used-artex-ai-to-steal-82-million-data-records/)</span>

## What to do this week

- Apply emergency security updates to PHP, Microsoft .NET, and Node.js environments to mitigate active Brotli exploits.
- Restrict external chat permissions in Microsoft Teams and remind staff never to download remote desktop utilities from unverified messages.
- Review online storefront codebases for unauthorized JavaScript injections and enable multi-factor authentication on administrative dashboards.
