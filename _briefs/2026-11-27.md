### SAP Confirms Cyberattack Involving Stolen GitHub Tokens

German software giant SAP has confirmed a cybersecurity incident after detecting suspicious activity within its development infrastructure and GitHub repositories. Threat actors reportedly used stolen employee tokens to access, copy, and potentially alter company source code. SAP stated that it took immediate action to secure its systems and protect customers.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/sap-germany-cyberattack-github), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sap-confirms-security-incident-after-suspicious-github-activity/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/sap-confirms-cyberattack-source-code-reportedly-compromised/)</span>

### AI Agents Used in Cyberattacks on Swiss Critical Infrastructure

Switzerland's national cybersecurity agency confirmed that attackers leveraging AI agents breached computer systems across four organizations, including three critical infrastructure operators. These mark the first documented instances of AI-driven cyber intrusions resulting in compromised systems in the country.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/switzerland-confirms-ai-assisted.html)</span>

### Drupal Warns of Active Exploitation of Critical Site Takeover Flaw

The Drupal security team issued an urgent warning regarding a critical vulnerability that attackers are actively exploiting in the wild. The flaw enables threat actors to completely hijack unpatched websites without authorization. System administrators are urged to apply the latest security patches immediately to protect their deployments.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/drupal-warns-of-new-access-bypass-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/)</span>

### Severe 12-Year-Old NFS Flaw Grants Root Access on Linux Systems

Cybersecurity researchers disclosed a critical 12-year-old vulnerability in the Linux kernel's Network File System server, tracked as CVE-2026-99362. If exploited, the flaw allows remote attackers to escalate privileges and gain full root control over vulnerable systems. System administrators should verify their kernel configurations and apply available security updates promptly.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-nfs2root-linux-flaw-can-let-attackers-gain-root-privileges/)</span>

### New Phishing Campaigns and Malicious Extensions Target Corporate Logins

Threat actors are employing sophisticated lures to hijack email and corporate credentials. A phishing campaign named GoodGuys impersonates major brands with fake paid interview offers to steal Google and Okta logins, while the Remus infostealer relies on a fake Google Sheets browser extension to extract private Gmail messages.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/apple/fake-paid-expert-interviews-impersonate-brands-to-steal-google-logins/), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/remus-malware-steals-gmail-messages-via-fake-google-sheets-extension/)</span>

## What to do this week

- Update Drupal installations and Linux kernel packages to their latest patched releases.
- Audit installed browser extensions and immediately remove any untrusted or unexpected add-ons.
- Verify the sender of unsolicited expert interview or webinar invitations before clicking links.
- Revoke unused developer access tokens and enforce strict access controls on source repositories.
