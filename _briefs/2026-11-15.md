### Trojanized Claude Extension Found on Cursor Marketplace

Cursor confirmed an intruder compromised its extension marketplace and published a trojanized version of Anthropic's Claude Code extension. The breach occurred after an access key issued to AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI was stolen and abused by attackers. Developers using Cursor should inspect their installed plugins, remove unauthorized extensions, and rotate any sensitive API keys used in their development environments.

### Italy Fines Meta Over Android Photo Gallery Access

Italy's data protection authority penalized Meta more than €800 million for automatically accessing Android photo galleries without explicit user consent. Regulators found that Facebook's default photo and video suggestion feature violated personal privacy safeguards governing smartphones. Mobile users should check their device settings to ensure social media apps only have access to selected photos rather than entire media libraries.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/italy-fines-meta-805-million-over.html), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/meta-fined-887-million-in-italy-over-android-gallery-photos-and-videos-on-facebook/)</span>

### US Disrupts Massive North Korean Crypto Phishing Ring

United States authorities disrupted a prolific North Korean social engineering campaign tracked as FaceStealer. The campaign was reportedly responsible for stealing over $400 million in cryptocurrency by deceiving targets. Individuals and organizations handling digital assets should remain cautious of unsolicited direct messages and unverified software shared via online platforms.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/us-disrupts-north-korean-campaign-behind-400-million-crypto-theft/)</span>

### CISA Warns of Active Exploits Targeting UnrealIRCd Servers

The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency added a high-severity command injection vulnerability impacting UnrealIRCd to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. The flaw allows remote attackers to execute arbitrary server-level commands on vulnerable open-source chat servers. System administrators operating UnrealIRCd should immediately apply available updates to safeguard their infrastructure from compromise.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/attackers-exploit-unrealircd-flaw-that.html)</span>

## What to do this week

- Audit your IDE extensions in Cursor and remove unverified or suspicious tools, especially AI-related add-ons.
- Review mobile app permissions on Android and iOS to limit app access to your camera roll and personal files.
- Patch any UnrealIRCd server deployments immediately to block active command-injection exploits.
- Exercise heightened caution with direct messages, job offers, or unvetted software downloads involving cryptocurrency.
